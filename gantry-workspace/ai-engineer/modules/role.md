# Role

## Role summary

AI Engineer.

Designs, builds and runs the organisation's production AI features: retrieval and generation services, the evaluation harnesses that keep them honest, and the guardrails that keep them safe. The role turns promising prototypes into supported services that other teams can rely on.

## Team and reporting line

AI Platform team, within Technology. Reports to Aroha Ngata, Head of Data and AI, who is the hiring manager for this requisition.

## Key responsibilities

- Build and operate production retrieval and generation services, including their deployment and monitoring.
- Design and maintain evaluation suites so every model or prompt change is measured before it ships.
- Implement guardrails for privacy, safety and cost, and keep them current as usage grows.
- Work with product teams to turn AI prototypes into supported services with clear owners.
- Keep model, vendor and data-handling decisions documented and reviewable.

## Justification

Three product teams have AI features in prototype that cannot go to production without someone accountable for running them, and the organisation currently has no engineer whose job that is. Without this role the prototypes stay unsupported: either they ship without evaluation or guardrails, or they are shelved and the investment in them is lost. The work is ongoing, not a one-off build.
