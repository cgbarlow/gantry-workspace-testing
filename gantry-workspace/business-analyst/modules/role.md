# Role

## Role summary

**Business Analyst**

The Business Analyst works with product owners and delivery teams to understand business needs, map current processes, and turn requirements into clear, testable specifications for digital services.

## Team and reporting line

Digital Delivery, Service Design and Delivery team. Reports to the Delivery Lead. Hiring manager: Jane Example (Delivery Lead).

## Key responsibilities

- Elicit and document business requirements with stakeholders
- Map current and future state processes
- Write user stories and acceptance criteria
- Support testing and validation of delivered changes
- Maintain traceability between requirements and delivery
- Facilitate workshops and prioritisation sessions

## Justification

Two major service improvement projects start in Q1 2027 with no dedicated BA capacity. Without this role, requirements work falls to product owners and developers, adding an estimated 3 to 4 months to delivery and increasing rework risk.
